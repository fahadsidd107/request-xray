# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Request X-Ray is an open-source developer community. Everyone participating in project spaces is expected to act respectfully and constructively, regardless of experience, background, identity, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be welcoming, patient, considerate, and open to technical disagreement.
- Critique ideas, code, and evidence rather than people.
- Respect privacy and avoid disclosing private information.
- Assume good intent while taking reported harm seriously.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, intimidation, discriminatory remarks, sexualized harassment, sustained disruption, stalking, doxxing, and retaliation against people raising concerns are not permitted.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, project chat, events, and other spaces where someone represents Request X-Ray.

## Reporting and enforcement

**Before inviting public contributions, maintainers must publish a private conduct-reporting contact in `docs/MAINTAINERS.md`.** Do not submit sensitive conduct reports to a public GitHub issue. Until that contact exists, public community intake should not be advertised as operational.

Maintainers will acknowledge reports privately, review relevant context, restrict access to reports, avoid conflicts of interest, and decide proportionate actions (warning, content removal, temporary suspension, or permanent ban). Reports involving a maintainer should be handled by another uninvolved maintainer where available. There is no guaranteed response-time commitment until maintainers publish one.

## Attribution

This policy is original project wording informed by common open-source community standards; it is not a claim of adopting a specific upstream code-of-conduct version.
